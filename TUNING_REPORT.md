## What this volume does and does not prove

These 270 runs DO prove that, inside the kernel's own model of the world, the documented invariants hold (or fail, above) across adversarial and random inputs far beyond the 26 hand-written unit tests: no execution without capability + owner authority, no spend past a hard budget ceiling, receipt chains that verify end to end, ledgers that reconcile to the receipt, and a dial that content cannot move. Volume here buys breadth of input coverage and regression protection: any future change that breaks an invariant will break loudly, with a seed and a repro attached.

It does NOT prove production readiness. Execution in this harness is a governed record, not real tool side effects (D-007); no real module (CNS, router, control plane) is integrated; the injection screen is a pattern list, and paraphrases outside the list are a known open flank, not a solved problem; concurrency, crash recovery, suffix deletion of the receipt chain, and performance are all untested. Simulation numbers are simulation numbers. Evidence state after this pass: Slice 1 + Slice 2 remain PARTIALLY PROVEN — with materially wider local evidence.
